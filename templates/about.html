About the Project

E-Cycle — Smart E-Waste Management System

Problem Statement

Electronic waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams. Most people throw old electronics into ordinary household waste, where hazardous materials leak into the environment and valuable resources are lost. There is a need for a simple system that helps people dispose of e-waste responsibly.

Project Objectives

  • Provide an easy way to submit e-waste collection requests.
  • Automatically segregate items into reusable, repairable, recyclable, and special-handling categories.
  • Promote reuse, repair, and responsible recycling over disposal.
  • Demonstrate the circular economy in a working prototype.

Proposed Solution

E-Cycle is a web application where users submit details of their old electronics. A rule-based engine classifies each item and recommends the right handling path. Administrators can track requests, update their status, and export data.

Technologies Used

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Chart.js, Font Awesome
  • Backend: Python with Flask
  • Database: SQLite

Innovation & Circular Economy Contribution

The project applies the circular economy principle — Reduce → Reuse → Repair → Refurbish → Recycle → Recover Materials — by extending the life of electronics and directing unusable items to authorised recyclers instead of landfills.

Future Scope

  • Pickup scheduling and route optimisation
  • Partnerships with authorised recyclers
  • Image-based classification of e-waste
  • Map integration for collection points
  • Machine learning for smarter segregation
