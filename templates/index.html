Why Not the Household Bin?

Electronic waste contains hazardous materials and valuable resources that ordinary waste systems cannot handle safely.

Hazardous Materials

Devices contain lead, mercury, cadmium, and flame retardants. When dumped in landfills, these can leak into soil and groundwater.

Valuable Resources

Gold, silver, copper, and rare metals in electronics can be recovered and reused — reducing the need for new mining.
