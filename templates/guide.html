Segregation Guide

Learn how different types of e-waste should be handled safely and responsibly.

Reduce unnecessary electronic purchases → Reuse → Repair → Refurbish → Recycle → Recover Materials
Safety warning: Never burn e-waste or dismantle batteries and electronic devices at home. Burning releases toxic fumes, and opening batteries can cause fires, leaks, or injury.

Reusable Items

Recommended action: Reuse, donate, or refurbish.

Examples: Working phones, laptops, tablets, desktops.

Precautions: Wipe personal data before donating. If no longer needed, hand to an authorised collection point.

Repairable Items

Recommended action: Professional repair first.

Examples: Devices with minor faults — cracked screens, faulty batteries, loose ports.

Precautions: Use qualified repair services. If repair is impractical, send to authorised recycling.

Recyclable Items

Recommended action: Authorised recycling for material recovery.

Examples: Damaged devices, chargers, cables, TVs, monitors.

Precautions: Handle carefully; never break or burn. Deliver to an authorised e-waste facility.

Special Handling

Recommended action: Always separate batteries.

Examples: All batteries — phone, laptop, button cells, power banks.

Precautions: Never open, crush, burn, or throw in household waste. Store cool and dry, then take to an authorised battery collection point.
